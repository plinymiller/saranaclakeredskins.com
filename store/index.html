Questions

Good to know

What does "100% of proceeds" mean?

Everything left after the cost of making and shipping each item goes to the cause you chose. Nobody keeps a cent. We will post totals raised for each cause.

When can I check out?

Very soon. You can build your bag now and it will be waiting for you. Secure payment is being set up and will open shortly.

How long does an order take?

Items are made to order. Plan on about two to three weeks for apparel and hats, and about a week for posters.

Can I return or exchange?

Because every item is made to order and the proceeds go to charity, sales are final, but if something arrives damaged or wrong we will make it right. Contact us.

Is this official school merchandise?

No. This is an independent alumni fundraiser. Questions: plinymiller@gmail.com.
