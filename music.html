    Name the players

    Were you in the band?

    Help us credit everyone: the director, the drum major, the soloists and every player by section. Email plinymiller@gmail.com, or use the “Add names” link under any clip.

    1. Tell us which clip (or the event and year), your name and your class year.
    2. List who you remember, with their instrument. Partial lists help too.
    3. Have the song title, a program or a photo? Send it along.
    4. We check the names before they go up. Ask any time and we will take yours off.
