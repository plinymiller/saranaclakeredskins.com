Football 1929

The 1930 yearbook calls 1929 the high point of the program's first seven years: a fifth straight Adirondack League title and a 6-6 tie with Franklin Academy; 5-1-2, with the only loss to Northwood School.

Read the full text (public domain)

Football reached the highest point of its seven year career at Saranac Lake High School during the season of 1929, when the Red and White gridmen not only won their fifth consecutive Adirondack League championship but went on to tie for the mythical supremacy of Northern New York by holding Franklin Academy of Malone to a 6-6 deadlock. Incidentally, this was the first time in four years that a Saranac eleven has not succumbed to the powerful Green and White teams from the North.

Graduation will be unusually severe with the Red and White squad as only two of the first string men will be on hand for practice next fall. Besides "Walt" Finnegan, erstwhile leader of the 1929 aggregation, "Bob" Downs, "Joe" Beiderbecke, "Bill" Hopkins, Harris Ryan, "Chuck" Bowker, "Wally" Herron, "Tag" Tagliabue and "Mil" Deitz will receive their diplomas this June. Captain-elect "Bud" Malone and "Ed." Plummer, alone of the regular eleven will return for 1930.
