The 5th Quarter

How the Saranac Lake away-game tailgate began, as told by Ann (Annie) Fina, who started it with Sue Dwyer and with help from Fr. Emil of St. Joe’s.

Started by
Ann (Annie) Fina and Sue Dwyer
Helped by
Fr. Emil, St. Joe’s (St. Joseph’s)
First tailgate
An away game in Moriah
“Sue Dwyer and I started it because we played in Moriah and we knew there were no restaurants there but they never told us they loaded up the snack bar with food to feed us. They were pissed but that was our first tailgate and we all liked it so we just continued it for every away game. We’d call everyone each week on what to bring. Fr. Emil donated a lot of stuff from St. Joe’s like meat and grills.”Ann (Annie) Fina[1]

How it started

The 5th Quarter began as a tailgate for Saranac Lake football away games. Ann (Annie) Fina and Sue Dwyer started it on a trip to play in Moriah. They knew there were no restaurants there, so they brought food. What they did not know was that Moriah had already loaded the snack bar to feed the visitors.

That first gathering became the first 5th Quarter tailgate. People were surprised on both sides, but the group liked it enough to keep going — and they did, for every away game after that. Each week they called around to decide what to bring.

Fr. Emil of St. Joe’s (St. Joseph’s) donated a great deal to the effort, including meat and grills. Together, Ann Fina, Sue Dwyer and Fr. Emil formed the 5th Quarter.

On the Football pages

This site’s 1985 football season notes already record that the 5th Quarter Club booster group was founded that year, and that it built a new press box and concession stand in 1987.[2]

Annie’s account does not give a year for that first tailgate in Moriah, so this page does not assign one.
