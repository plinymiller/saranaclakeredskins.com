The 5th Quarter

How the Saranac Lake away-game tailgate began, as told by Ann Fina, who started it with Sue Dwyer and with help from Fr. Emil of St. Joe’s.

Started by
Ann Fina and Sue Dwyer
Helped by
Fr. Emil, St. Joe’s (St. Joseph’s)
First tailgate
An away game in Moriah
“Sue Dwyer and I started it because we played in Moriah and we knew there were no restaurants there but they never told us they loaded up the snack bar with food to feed us. They were pissed but that was our first tailgate and we all liked it so we just continued it for every away game. We’d call everyone each week on what to bring. Fr. Emil donated a lot of stuff from St. Joe’s like meat and grills.”Ann Fina[1]

How it started

The 5th Quarter began as a tailgate for Saranac Lake football away games. Ann Fina and Sue Dwyer started it on a trip to play in Moriah. They knew there were no restaurants there, so they brought food. What they did not know was that Moriah had already loaded the snack bar to feed the visitors.

That first gathering became the first 5th Quarter tailgate. People were surprised on both sides, but the group liked it enough to keep going — and they did, for every away game after that. Each week they called around to decide what to bring.

Fr. Emil of St. Joe’s (St. Joseph’s) donated a great deal to the effort, including meat and grills. Together, Ann Fina, Sue Dwyer and Fr. Emil formed the 5th Quarter.

On the Football pages

This site’s 1985 football season notes already record that the 5th Quarter Club booster group was founded that year, and that it built a new press box and concession stand in 1987.[2]

Ann’s account does not give a year for that first tailgate in Moriah, so this page does not assign one.

The club today

The 5th Quarter Club is still active. In a May 2018 letter to the Adirondack Daily Enterprise, the club wrote that it was established in 1985 by a group of enthusiastic parents, and that what began with tailgate parties at Homecoming games had grown into a volunteer group of hometown football enthusiasts supporting the Saranac Lake football program.[3]

Ann Fina’s account places that first tailgate at an away game in Moriah. The 2018 letter describes Homecoming tailgates at the club’s conception. This page records both and does not treat them as one event.

By 2018 the club said it still held tailgates, and that it also provided lunches for away games, sponsored two PAL football players, organized a varsity banquet, held breakfasts during playoff season, purchased items the football program needed, and paid for HUDL, which benefits the coaches and the players.[3]

Its fundraising, the letter said, includes an annual golf tournament whose proceeds benefit the entire Saranac Lake football program; ads in the football programs sold at home games; Red Storm accessories sold at the club’s “Red Tent” at home games; and running the concession when needed. The club is volunteer-run and said it is always looking for new members.[3]

The golf tournament

Public thank-you letters place the club’s annual golf tournament at the Saranac Lake Golf Club in Ray Brook. An August 2013 letter noted that the club had recently held its first annual tournament.[4] The fifth annual tournament was held on Saturday, June 10, 2017, at the Saranac Lake Golf Course; that year’s letter was signed by Allen Moody as president of the 5th Quarter Club.[5]

In 2022, the first-place team was Niederbuhl Bros. (Derek Strack, Tristan Fitzgerald, Daniel Strack and Mike Rice). The thank-you letter, signed by Debbie Moody, also thanked Tony and the staff at the Saranac Lake Golf Club, Greg Landon for helping on the grill, and named Allen, Tami, Aleacia and Debbie among those organizing the day.[6]

In 2024, the first-place team was Wayne Darrah Auto Body (Jay Branch, Dennis Gallagher, Brady Roberts and Will Woodruff). That letter was signed by Allen Moody, Tami Donaldson, Aleacia Landon and Debbie Moody.[7]

A July 8, 2025, letter, as republished from the Adirondack Daily Enterprise, congratulated the first-place team sponsored by Rock Solid Patios and thanked hole sponsors, beginning with Allen Moody Landscaping.[8] The full text of that letter could not be retrieved while this page was updated, so the rest of that year’s sponsors are not listed here.

The Saranac Lake Golf Club has listed the Annual Fifth Quarter Club Tournament as an 18-hole scramble on Saturday, June 13, starting at 9 a.m. at the Ray Brook course, with the course opening to the public at 2 p.m. The listing marks the event as having passed and does not print a year. The most recent Saturday, June 13, was in 2026.[9]

Support the Fifth Quarter Club

This update did not find a public PayPal, Venmo, GoFundMe, Givebutter, or school-foundation giving link published by the 5th Quarter Club, and the letters cited here do not include a mailing address or email for sending a check.

The ways the club itself has described supporting the program are to take part in the annual golf tournament, buy an ad in the home-game football program, shop the Red Tent at home games, help with concessions, or volunteer.[3]

The Historic Saranac Lake Wiki points readers to the Saranac Lake Football 5th Quarter Club on Facebook for current news.[10] This page could not read that Facebook page (the service blocked automated access), so it cannot confirm whether the page is still active or lists an online donation method.
