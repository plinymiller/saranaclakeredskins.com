1985 JV football

CVAC Division I champions

7-1-1 record · 267 points for, 59 against

#1988 JV

1985 Saranac Lake JV Football, the full season playlist · adkdon · YouTube

The season

The 1985 Saranac Lake junior varsity, mostly sophomores from the Class of 1988, went 7-1-1 and won the CVAC Division I title. Coach Jeff Cheney: “Blanchard and Fina were two fine quarterbacks.” Eight of the nine games survive on home-video tape. The film is an ADKDon Production · DKF69FILMS.

Scores come from the Adirondack Daily Enterprise. The games were matched to the film by scoreboards, uniforms, fields and the KBAD TV commentary. Corrections welcome: contact us.
