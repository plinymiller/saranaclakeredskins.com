Write-ups

Adirondack Daily Enterprise · Oct 10, 1977

Redskins blank Canton (Steve Cohen)

Pete Camelo ran for 142 yards and a TD and Tod Fobare threw a fourth-down TD to Mario Brindisi in a 14-0 win at Canton — the third straight shutout — in a game remembered for Coach Raymond being flagged when the wind carried his tossed hat onto the field.

“Monday is Columbus Day, not Flag Day!”

Adirondack Daily Enterprise · Oct 31, 1977

Shutout streak hits 6 (Steve Cohen)

Tod Fobare threw for 191 yards and three touchdowns, with Dan Capone catching two, as the Redskins beat St. Lawrence Central 25-0 at Petrova Field for a sixth straight shutout.

Adirondack Daily Enterprise · Nov 7, 1977

'Skins '77 NAC champs (Steve Cohen)

A 33-12 win over Potsdam completed an 8-0 season, the unblemished campaign Coach John Raymond's program had chased for five years (1973-77: 35-3-2 per the story).
