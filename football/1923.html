The Republican-Journal (Ogdensburg) · Oct 17, 1923

Tupper Lake preview (mentions Saranac Lake loss)

An Ogdensburg preview of Tupper Lake's game with St. Mary's notes that in its only other game of the season Tupper Lake beat Saranac Lake 12-0 — one of the earliest newspaper mentions of a Saranac Lake High School football result.
