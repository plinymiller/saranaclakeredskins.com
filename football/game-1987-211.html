Write-ups

Adirondack Daily Enterprise · Nov 9, 1987

Saranac Lake ousts St. John's for Super Bowl bid (Tom Keegan)

Chris Blanchard's touchdown pass to Steve McCormick produced a 7-0 win over St. John's on the day the field was dedicated as Ken Wilson Field, clinching the Division I title and a North Country Super Bowl bid.

“The Redskins are on their way to the North Country Super Bowl.”

Adirondack Daily Enterprise (Nancy DeLong), transcribed on the HSL wiki · Oct 23, 1987

SL football field to be named for Wilson

The school board unanimously approved naming the Petrova field for longtime coach and superintendent Ken Wilson, at the request of the Classes of 1935-41 reunion committee led by Donald Dupree; the dedication was set for the Nov. 7 home finale.

“It was almost carnival-like”
