Sources and credits

This section was put together from public archives, old newspapers, school yearbooks and game film shared online. Thank you to everyone who kept these records.

Historic Saranac Lake wiki

Historic Saranac Lake’s wiki was our richest source: its Football page, player and coach biographies, transcribed newspaper clippings, the Coogan scrapbooks, and scans of the Canaras yearbooks. Every game, roster and bio drawn from it links back to the page it came from. Yearbook photos published before 1931 are public domain and are shown here. Later photos stay on the wiki, and we link to them with credit.

NYS Historic Newspapers

Scores and stories from 1923 to the 2000s were found in the digitized papers at NYS Historic Newspapers, a project of the Northern New York Library Network and the New York State Library. Many scores were read by machine (OCR) from small-type scoreboard lists. Those carry an unverified tag until someone checks them by hand.

How we handle copyright

  • Articles and yearbook pages published before 1931 are in the US public domain, and we show their full text.
  • For anything newer we give a short summary, at most one brief quote, and a link to the original.
  • Newspaper and wiki photos published after 1930 are linked, not copied, and credited to their source.

How to read the data

  • unverified: a score read by machine from an old scoreboard list, or found in only one secondary source.
  • Sources disagree: when two sources give different facts, we show both instead of picking one.
  • Where a season record is marked “in games found so far”, some games from that year are still missing.
