Adirondack League champions

Season notes

  • Sixth straight Adirondack League title. At a Jan 1931 banquet for 160 at the Hotel Saranac, 14 letter winners got miniature gold footballs; captain Howard Malone. Only three veterans returned from 1929. source

Write-ups

Adirondack Daily Enterprise (probably; Coogan scrapbook clipping on the HSL wiki) · Jan 5, 1931

Banquet pays honor to grid 11's successes

160 townspeople honored the 1930 team, winner of a sixth straight Adirondack Interscholastic League title, at the Hotel Saranac; each of the 14 letter winners received a miniature gold football. The story names every letterman by position and remembers former player Harris H. Ryan, who had just died.
