Write-ups

Adirondack Daily Enterprise · Nov 3, 1975

Therein, the wind and the win! (Helen Ward)

In biting cold, wind and rain at Malone, the Redskins shut out ninth-ranked Franklin Academy 6-0 to clinch Saranac Lake's first Northern Athletic Conference championship.

“It was the best of times on the worst of days.”
