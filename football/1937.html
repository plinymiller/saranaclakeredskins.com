Canaras (SLHS yearbook), 1938 — text on the HSL wiki · 1938-06

Football — 1937 season

With an almost entirely new line and backfield, the 1937 team opened by beating Malone 19-6 after trailing at the half, then struggled with injuries. Highlights included Rube Skeels' 70-yard TD run and Hugh Kelly's long scoring runs against Canton and Lake Placid.
