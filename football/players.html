Players and coaches

Short bios drawn mostly from the Historic Saranac Lake wiki, Canaras yearbooks and newspaper stories. Each one links to its source. Know someone who belongs here? Contact us.

Players

Tom Wallace “Galloping Ghost”

FB · Class of 1928

Seasons: 1924, 1925, 1926, 1927

Scored 11 touchdowns (66 points) against Lake Placid on Oct 16, 1926, and 24 TDs that season. Nicknamed the 'Galloping Ghost'. Later captain at Georgetown, then a lawyer and Franklin County probation officer; died 1969.

  • A. H. Denny cup MVP 1926
  • SLHS Sports Hall of Fame 1987
  • State hurdles champion

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Anthony B. Gedroiz “Tony”

QB

Seasons: 1923

Quarterback of the first SLHS team; scored a touchdown in the first game, 20-0 over Mineville, on Oct 6, 1923. Later a physician.

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Cornelius J. Carey Jr.

Manager · Class of 1926

Seasons: 1923, 1924, 1925

Football manager for the first three seasons; later business manager of Notre Dame football and a county judge.

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Bob Herron

HB · Class of 1926

Seasons: 1923, 1924, 1925

Captained the 1924 and 1925 teams; the 1925 team went unscored upon (180-0). Called the class of '26's greatest athlete.

  • Captain 1924-25

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Walter Herron “Wally”

HB, E · Class of 1930

Seasons: 1926, 1927, 1928, 1929

'Wally', the lightest man on the 1927 squad, was remembered as the greatest halfback of Ken Wilson's early years. Letter winner again in 1930 per the banquet story.

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Harold L. Hill

T · Class of 1928

Seasons: 1925, 1926, 1927

Tackle and captain of the 1927 Adirondack League champions (201-30).

  • Captain 1927
  • Adirondack champions 1927

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Thomas Daley

E · Class of 1928

Seasons: 1925, 1926, 1927

End on three teams, 1925-27; profiled in the 1927 'Beat Malone' sport supplement.

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Ed Toohey

Line · Class of 1926

Seasons: 1923, 1924, 1925

Three-year player on the first teams; probably the 'Ed Twokey' in the 1923 newspaper lineup.

  • A. H. Denny Football Trophy 1924

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Walter Finnegan

T · Class of 1930

Seasons: 1926, 1927, 1928, 1929

Captained the 1929 team: fifth straight Adirondack title and a 6-6 tie with Malone.

  • Captain 1929

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Howard 'Bud' Malone

E · Class of 1931

Seasons: 1929, 1930

End; captain-elect in 1929, captain of the 1930 team, the sixth straight Adirondack League champions.

  • Captain 1930

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Hayward 'Red' Plumadore “Red”

T · Class of 1933

Seasons: 1929, 1930, 1931, 1932

All-Adirondack tackle and captain; went on to St. Lawrence; wrestled professionally as the 'Masked Marvel'.

  • All-Adirondack tackle
  • Captain
  • SLHS Sports Hall of Fame 1987
  • 1940 US Olympic wrestling team

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Carl J. Ryan

Seasons: 1931, 1932

His obituary says he captained the Redskins in 1931 and 1932; the 1933 Canaras names Plumadore as 1932 captain (conflict).

  • Captain 1931 and 1932 (obituary)

More on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki ↗

Harris H. Ryan

G · Class of 1930

Seasons: 1927, 1928, 1929

Guard, 1927-29; died on New Year's Eve 1930 and was remembered at the Jan 1931 team banquet.

Pat Hickey

Seasons: 1935

1935 captain; hurt in the Plattsburgh game.

Hugh L. Kelly

HB · Class of 1938

Seasons: 1934, 1935, 1936, 1937

Four-year player and senior-year captain known for long scoring runs. Died in 1942 in World War II; the 1943 Canaras was dedicated to him.

  • Captain 1937
