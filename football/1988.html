Write-ups

Adirondack Daily Enterprise · Nov 21, 1988

Confident Redskins ground Cyclones, 21-12 (Jim Stowell)

A year after losing 26-0, Saranac Lake beat Watertown 21-12 at Watertown High to win North Country Super Bowl X and end the Cyclones' 19-game winning streak; Dennis Dwyer was named MVP.

“A big part of this game was revenge. It is sweet.”
