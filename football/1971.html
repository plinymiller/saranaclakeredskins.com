Adirondack Daily Enterprise · Oct 18, 1971

Derailed Redskins Retain Admiration (Bill McLaughlin)

Bill McLaughlin's page-one story on the Mayor's Trophy loss to Tupper Lake praised the Redskins' effort before nearly 4,000 fans; a late interception by Tupper's John Tice stopped Saranac Lake's go-ahead try. Howard Riley revisited the story in a 2023 Enterprise column.

“This gives one an idea how good a team can look in losing.”
