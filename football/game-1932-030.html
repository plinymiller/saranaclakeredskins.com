Canaras (SLHS yearbook), 1933 · 1933-06

Football — 1932 season

The yearbook describes a 1932 season with two real victories, three ties and a loss to Potsdam in a substitute championship game, capped by a 13-6 win at Malone; league officials declined to award a title that year after a rules infraction.
