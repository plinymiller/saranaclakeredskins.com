SLHS mimeographed 'Sport Supplement' — 'BEAT MALONE' (Coogan scrapbooks, HSL wiki) · 1927-11

Player profiles before the Malone game

A student-made football supplement before the 1927 Malone game profiled the regulars. A handwritten note records that the 1927 team won the Adirondack championship but lost to Malone 18-7.

Read the full text (public domain)

Captain Harold Hill, Tackle

This year's captain has been a great performer for the Saranac Lake High team. The lanky tackle is quick on his feet, and is adept at breaking the enemy's defense. 'Charlie' is truly a clever player, of three years experience, and a remarkably good kicker. He has been one of the shining lights on the offense this year. Its tough he leaves us this year." In a paragraph titled "What the Big Guns Have to Say," Capt. Harold "Charlie" Hill is quoted: "We cannot win alone. We need the backing of the student body. It is the cheers from the sidelines that instill the fighting spirit into the players which urges them to win. It is not going to be an easy game and our real chance lies with the student body, whether you back us with the right spirit or not.

Tom Daley, End

Walter Herron, Halfback or End — 'Wally' is the lightest man on the squad but due to his great fighting spirit he became a regular. He is a good tackler and not many got around his end. When in the backfield, he hit the line like a veteran. Being a junior, 'Wally' is a bright prospect for next year's team.

Canaras (SLHS yearbook), 1928 — scan on the HSL wiki · 1928-06

The Football Squad — line-up and season's record

The 1928 yearbook lists the 1927 squad by position and the full season: 6-1-1 counting a 6-6 Alumni game, 201 points to 30, with the only loss at Malone. Harold Hill captained the team.

Read the full text (public domain)

LINE-UP

Ends: Paul Clancy, James Munn, Walter Herron, Tuffield Latour, Thomas Daley, Raymond Horiwitz. Tackles: Harold Hill, William Wallace, Phelps Utting, Walter Finnegan, Eugene Miller, Robert Downs, Jack McGrath. Centers: Hall Parker, Gilson Slater, Charles Bowker, Joseph Knapp. Guards: Harris Ryan, Bernard Dupree, Milford Deitz, William Hopkins. Quarterbacks: Thomas Kunz, William Natter. Fullbacks: Thomas Wallace, George Scollins. Halfbacks: George Munn, William Cavanaugh, Orville LaBarge, Harold Gallup.

SEASON'S RECORD

Saranac Lake 6, Alumni 6; Saranac Lake 32, Lake Placid 0; Saranac Lake 66, Alexandria Bay 0; Saranac Lake 26, Plattsburg 0; Saranac Lake 25, Ticonderoga 6; Saranac Lake 39, Tupper Lake 0; Saranac Lake 7, Malone 18. Saranac Lake 201, Opponents 30.
