Where the names come from

  • Graduate lists and graduation photo pages printed each June in the Adirondack Daily Enterprise (NYS Historic Newspapers). These were machine-read (OCR), so spellings can be off.
  • Biographies and lists on the Historic Saranac Lake wiki.
  • Nothing is guessed. If a list was too garbled to read safely, the class page links to the scan instead.

Missing or misspelled? Email plinymiller@gmail.com.
