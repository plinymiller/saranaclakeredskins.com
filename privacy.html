Saranac Lake Redskins

Privacy

saranaclakeredskins.com is an independent alumni project for Saranac Lake High School classes of 1890 to 2000. It is not an official site of the school district. Questions about this policy, or requests to have your account and details removed, can be sent to plinymiller@gmail.com.

What we collect

When you create an account we ask for your first name, last name, name at graduation (if different), a username, email address, cell phone number, and an optional mailing address (street, city, state, and ZIP). You also choose a password and tell us whether to list your name and city, email, or cell number in the classmate directory. If you post on a bulletin board, we store the message you write. If you send class photos to us, we may store and display the image and any caption you provide.

How it is stored

Account, directory, message, and photo information is stored with Supabase, a cloud database and sign-in service we use to run the site. Your password is handled by that service; we do not keep it in plain text. Your browser also holds a sign-in session so you can stay logged in.

Who can see it

Class organizers can see the contact details you provide so they can reach you about class news and reunions. Classmates see your email address or cell number only if you tick those boxes, and only after an organizer confirms that you belong to the class. Your name and city appear in the directory only if you choose to be listed. Bulletin board messages are visible to signed-in classmates on the board you post to. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it outside the class except as needed to operate the site or as required by law.

Removal and corrections

You can update your details while signed in. To have your account and details removed, or to correct something you cannot change yourself, email plinymiller@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the update on this page.
